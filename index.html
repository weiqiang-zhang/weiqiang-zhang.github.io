I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Freie Universität Berlin, where I am supervised by Prof. Dr. Theocharis Grigoriadis at the Institute for East European Studies.

My primary research is in the political economy of institutions and development economics. I study how political institutions — ideological education, the enforcement of everyday rights, the design of state policy — shape economic behaviour, labor market outcomes, and local development, with an empirical focus on China. Methodologically I work with quasi-experimental designs on panel, administrative, and survey data.

Alongside this, I maintain an ongoing interest in energy and environmental economics, where most of my published work to date is situated, and which I approach from the same institutional angle: how carbon markets, energy transitions, and state policy translate into employment and firm behaviour.

Before Berlin I read for a double master's degree at Southeast University and Monash University, and worked as a research assistant at Shanxi University and Southeast University.
